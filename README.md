## Featured work

### 🤖 [AI Growth Agent: multi-agent prospecting system](https://github.com/surya-upputuru/true-digital-ai-growth-agent)
Two orchestrators and five sub-agents that find businesses with fixable growth gaps, score the opportunity, estimate ROI and draft personalised outreach. Every send is gated by a human.
- Cuts prospect research from **30–45 minutes to under 3 minutes of review**
- Groq → OpenRouter failover that has already handled live rate-limit failures in production
- Checks HubSpot before writing so no duplicate records, and Gmail is draft-only: nothing can send without approval

`n8n` `Firecrawl` `Groq` `OpenRouter` `HubSpot` `Gmail API` `Slack`

### 💬 [True Digital AI Assistant: RAG chat + voice](https://github.com/surya-upputuru/true-digital-ai-assistant)
A website assistant that visitors can type to or talk to. Both channels answer from one shared knowledge base and hand warm leads to the team.
- Retrieval-grounded answers, with hard guardrails on pricing and promises
- Only confirms a call back once the lead is actually saved to Supabase and HubSpot
- Model fallback (Groq → Gemini), with graceful handling when retrieval or lead capture fails

`n8n` `Supabase pgvector` `Gemini embeddings` `ElevenLabs` `HubSpot`

### 📈 [SEO case study: local dermatology clinic](https://github.com/surya-upputuru/Seo-Case-Study)

- **28,900 impressions and 365 clicks** in 3 months (Google Search Console)

`Technical SEO` `Local SEO` `Google Search Console` `On-page optimisation`
